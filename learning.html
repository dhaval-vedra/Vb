1. Platform Overview & Architectural Pillars

Vedra BI is an enterprise-grade analytics and visualization platform built with a local-first philosophy. Powered by an embedded in-memory DuckDB OLAP engine and Apache Arrow columnar binary IPC, it processes hundreds of thousands of records at 60 FPS directly in the browser with zero server roundtrips.

Reactive Grid Engine

Editing any cell inside the spreadsheet immediately pushes reactive updates to all bound KPI cards, charts, and computed formulas with zero page reloads.

Excel-Grade Formulas

Leverage 15 dynamic formulas like SUM, GROWTH, STATUS, and FORMAT to create responsive, calculated executive KPI cards identical to modern financial models.

Enterprise Security

Hardened with an 8-digit security PIN, automatic 30-minute inactivity lockout, and local encrypted session persistence for complete data privacy.

Direction Parameter Explanation (R vs. L):
R (Right): Displays the computed numerical value to the RIGHT of your label (e.g., "Revenue: $45,000").
L (Left): Displays the computed numerical value to the LEFT of your label (e.g., "$45,000 Revenue").

Multi-Sheet Parser

7. Detailed Guide: Chart Creation & Visual Taxonomy

Click the Charts icon in the main vertical sidebar to configure any of the 15+ analytical chart engines:

Bar Chart
Bar & Column Charts

Compare categorical dimensions such as sales by department, regional volume, or product line revenues.

Line / Area
Line & Area Charts

Analyze chronological trends over time, tracking continuous metrics like monthly revenue and rolling averages.

Pie / Donut
Pie & Donut Charts

Display proportional distribution, market share percentages, and categorical part-to-whole splits.

Scatter / Bubble
Scatter & Bubble Charts

Plot two or three independent continuous variables to discover correlations, clusters, and statistical outliers.

Radar Chart
Radar Charts

Evaluate multi-dimensional performance attributes across departments, products, or employee competency profiles.

Heatmap
Heatmap Matrix

Visualize density, activity intensity, and transaction volumes across day-of-week and hour-of-day matrices.

Gauge Meter
Gauge & Meter Charts

Display progress toward KPI targets on a 0–100% scale resembling an automotive speedometer dial.

Funnel & Treemap
Funnel & Treemap Charts

Examine conversion drop-offs along sales pipelines, or visualize hierarchical asset breakdowns with nested rectangular tiles.

Three Steps to Build Any Chart:
  1. 1. Select Dimension (X-Axis): Choose the categorical column to group records by (e.g., Region, Month).
  2. 2. Select Metric (Y-Axis): Choose the numerical column to compute (e.g., Sales, Margin, Volume).
  3. 3. Set Aggregation Method: Choose SUM (total), AVG (mean), or COUNT (frequency).

9. Detailed Guide: Dashboard Canvas, Grid Layouts & Views

Vedra BI provides a versatile, responsive workspace with precision grid alignment:

  • Freeform Placement & Resizing: Drag and drop cards anywhere on the canvas, resizing from edges and corners.
  • Snap-to-Grid (Ctrl+G): Toggle a magnetic alignment grid to maintain clean margins and uniform spacing.
  • One-Click Auto Arrange: Instantly organize cards into optimal 1-column, 2-column, or 3-column masonry arrangements.

  • Global Interactive Slicers: Filter by date ranges, quarters, or regions with instantaneous synchronization across all visuals.
