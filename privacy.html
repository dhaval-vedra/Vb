Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Vedra BI ("we," "us," "our," or "the Platform"). Vedra BI is a next-generation Business Intelligence, Visual Analytics, and KPI Reporting platform designed for data analysts, finance teams, operations leads, and corporate decision-makers.

This Privacy Policy outlines our uncompromising dedication to privacy, customer sovereignty, transparency, and data governance. It applies to all interactions with the Vedra BI web application, connected API endpoints, embedded chart viewers, documentation portals, and customer account services.

Core Operational Philosophy: We treat customer data as an absolute trust. Our software is architected so that analytical calculations, dataset filters, and visual rendering execute within your sandboxed browser environment whenever possible, minimizing server-side data persistence.
2

Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum amount of information required to deliver high-performance business intelligence services. Information falls into the following transparent categories:

Category Data Elements Collected Purpose & Legal Basis
Account & Identity Name, work email address, company/workspace identifier, profile avatar initials, and workspace role preferences. User authentication, workspace access control, collaborative sharing, and account communication.
Customer Datasets & Files Structured data files uploaded by you (CSV, Excel / XLSX, JSON payloads, or connected Google Sheets API references). Executing SQL aggregations, rendering charts, computing pivot metrics, and generating reports.
Security & Auth Tokens Cryptographic session identifiers, client-salted SHA-256 password/PIN digests, and multi-factor authorization timestamps. Zero-knowledge credential verification and unauthorized session prevention.
Telemetry & System Health Client browser type, screen resolution, query execution times, error stack traces, and feature usage counters. Performance optimization, diagnostic debugging, and preventing Denial-of-Service attacks.
Data We NEVER Collect:
  • We never collect sensitive personal records (e.g., government IDs, social security numbers, biometric records).
  • We never read or inspect unrelated files on your local file system.
  • We never log or transmit your raw, unencrypted passwords or security PINs.

How We Process & Utilize Data

Vedra BI uses collected information strictly for operational and analytical purposes outlined below:

In-Memory Analytics Engine

Our analytics pipeline executes via WebAssembly (DuckDB-WASM) and client-side memory. Your dataset rows are parsed in your browser context, delivering ultra-fast query speeds without storing raw data records on central servers.

Chart & KPI Visualization

Transforming structured metrics into bar charts, line graphs, geo heatmaps, scatter plots, and pivot tables based solely on your configured dimensions and metrics.

Security & Session Integrity

Validating user credentials, enforcing workspace role permissions, detecting brute-force intrusions, and verifying 8-digit security PIN challenges.

Export & Report Dispatch

Compiling your configured dashboard layout and charts into downloadable PDF, PNG, Excel, or JSON formats upon your explicit user trigger.

Data Ownership & Customer Rights

You Retain 100% Ownership of Your Business Data

You and your organization retain all right, title, and interest (including all intellectual property rights) in and to all datasets, spreadsheets, SQL schemas, business formulas, dashboards, and reports created within or uploaded to Vedra BI.

Our Binding Commitments:
  • No Data Monetization: We will never sell, lease, rent, trade, or monetize your raw data, aggregated metrics, or customer information to any third party, broker, or advertising network.
  • No Competitive Use: We do not use your business metrics or financial figures to develop competing products or commercial benchmarks.
  • Complete Portability: You can export all your workspace dashboards, layout configurations, and custom metadata at any time in standardized JSON and CSV formats.

Security Controls & Cryptographic Safeguards

We employ enterprise-grade administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect customer data against unauthorized access, accidental loss, or destruction.

In-Transit Encryption

All communications are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) with modern cipher suites.

At-Rest Protection

Stored configuration artifacts and tokens are safeguarded using AES-256 encryption standards.

Salted Cryptography

Credentials are secured with unique CSPRNG 16-byte cryptographic salts and one-way SHA-256 digests.

Multi-Factor 8-Digit Security PIN

Vedra BI supports an independent 8-digit security PIN layer. Even if a local browser session is left open, critical settings, workspace exports, and sensitive modifications require secondary salted-hash verification.

Data Retention, Portability & Right to Erasure

We adhere to strict data minimization principles. We retain account metadata only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with services.

Data Portability

You have the continuous right to export all custom dashboards, configured formulas, telemetry histories, and account details in portable, machine-readable JSON format with 1 click from the Profile Settings tab.

Right to Erasure (Complete Wipe)

You can trigger a permanent reset from the Profile Hub. This completely purges all local storage records, cached tokens, workspace preferences, and salted PIN hashes from your device.

Global Compliance (GDPR, CCPA / CPRA, SOC 2)

Vedra BI operates in accordance with worldwide privacy frameworks and data protection standards:

General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - EU & UK)

European Economic Area (EEA) and UK users have enforceable rights to access, rectify, restrict, object to processing, and erase their personal information. The legal basis for processing account data is contract fulfillment and legitimate operational security interests.

California Consumer Privacy Act & CPRA (CCPA - California, USA)

Under the CCPA/CPRA, California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt-out of any data sale. As stated, Vedra BI does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
